# antislop audit 001 (2026-11-04)

Mode: after. Direction: `DESIGN.md` (dark only, one electric green accent, Inter, dials ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2).

Scope: the React UI (`web/src`), the original page kept at `/legacy/` (`src/ui`), and the README.

Priority comes from the rule's tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Lock = LOW.
Nothing is changed until specific numbers are approved.

## Already clean

These checks found nothing: em dashes in UI text and docs (R-02), buzzwords (R-16), generic CTAs (R-15), icon
libraries or sparkle icons (R-04), gradients and glow in the app (R-01, R-13), and glassmorphism (R-10). Text
contrast is measured and recorded in `DESIGN.md` (R-25). Focus rings (R-32) and 44 px touch targets on

elevation (R-12).

## Not findings (reasons written down)

- **Dark only** (R-21): `DESIGN.md` gives the reason (footage reads best on dark, like a video editor). No toggle is needed.
- **Inter** (R-06): `DESIGN.md` gives the reason (legible numbers and small labels in a dense tool).
- **Live dot and pill** (`.live-tag`): a real recording status, in the reserved live red. This is a functional badge (R-09).
- **`.rv-caplines` and `.set-nav a` 2 px left border**: marks the current caption line or settings section. It's a selection state, not decoration.
- **"Discord ✓"** in Exports: a real sent/not-sent state.
