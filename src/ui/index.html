Twitch sign-in

Twitch puts ads into live streams for viewers without Turbo. Clip Radar spots those ad breaks and leaves them out of recordings either way. Sign in with an account that has Turbo (or a sub to the channel) and Twitch sends the ad-free stream instead, so recording never pauses for an ad.

Paste a sign-in token instead

On twitch.tv, signed in, press F12, open Application › Cookies › https://www.twitch.tv and copy the value of auth-token. It's stored encrypted for your Windows account and only sent to Twitch. Also used for subscriber-only VODs.

Clip ranking

Clip Radar learns from the clips you keep, skip and post, separately for each streamer, and moves moments like the ones you pick up the list. It starts after about 8 decisions. What it learned shows exactly what's stored and what it changes.

Live clipping

Works from any window while live clipping is running, so you can keep watching the stream or playing. You'll hear a short sound when the clip is taken.

Clean up automatically

Finished means: every suggested clip kept or skipped, every kept clip rendered, and no render waiting or failed (a retry needs the video). Only the downloaded video goes; your rendered clips and your review always stay.
