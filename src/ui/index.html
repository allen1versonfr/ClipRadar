TikTok only lets registered apps post. Setting one up is free and takes about 10 minutes.

  1. Go to developers.tiktok.com, sign in and create an app.
  2. Add the Login Kit and Content Posting API products, and turn on Direct Post.
  3. In Login Kit, choose the Desktop platform and add this redirect URI:
  4. Paste the app's Client key and Client secret below, then connect your account.

Until TikTok reviews your app, direct posts are limited to private (Only me). Sending to drafts works either way; you finish the post in the TikTok app. Your sign-in is stored encrypted for your Windows account.
