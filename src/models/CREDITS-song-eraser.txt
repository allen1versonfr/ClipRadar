Song eraser (Export VOD and Segments, "Erase song"): TIGER for Divide and Remaster

The model Clip Radar downloads for "Erase song" (saved as song_eraser.onnx) is TIGER, its Divide and Remaster
model (dialogue / music / effects), as converted to ONNX with int8 weights by audiojs.

  TIGER: Time-frequency Interleaved Gain Extraction and Reconstruction for Efficient Speech Separation
  Mohan Xu, Kai Li, Guo Chen, Xiaolin Hu (Tsinghua University), ICLR 2025, arXiv:2410.01469
  Code                        https://github.com/JusperLee/TIGER
  Weights                     https://huggingface.co/JusperLee/TIGER-DnR
  ONNX (what's downloaded)    https://huggingface.co/audiojs/tiger-dnr  (tiger.int8.onnx)
