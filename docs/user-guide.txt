VodClipper
==========
Turns a Twitch VOD into vertical TikTok clips. It finds the best moments,
lets you review and trim them, then renders 1080x1920 MP4s with captions.
Everything runs on your own PC.

INSTALL
  Download VodClipper-Setup.exe from the repo's Releases page and run it.
  It installs VodClipper for your user (no admin prompt) and adds Start menu
  and desktop shortcuts. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the
  app isn't signed: More info > Run anyway.

  No-install option: VodClipper-portable.zip. Unzip anywhere and run
  VodClipper.exe. To remove it, delete the folder.

UPDATES
  VodClipper checks GitHub for a new version when it starts (and every few
  hours). When there is one, "Update to x.y.z" appears in the status bar.
  Settings > Updates shows what's new; Update now downloads it, closes
  VodClipper, installs it and opens VodClipper again. Your clips, projects and
  settings are kept. Turn off "Check for updates automatically" there if you
  prefer to check yourself. The portable version links to the download page.
  If VodClipper's GitHub repository is private, the updater needs a read-only
  access token: Settings > Updates > Private repository explains how to make
  one. It's stored encrypted for your Windows account.

UNINSTALL
  Any of these:
    - Settings > Apps > Installed apps > VodClipper > Uninstall
    - Start menu > Uninstall VodClipper
    - In VodClipper: Settings > About > Uninstall
  It removes the app, its downloaded tools, caption models and logs, then asks
  whether to delete your VODs and clips too (it keeps them unless you say Yes).

BUILD IT YOURSELF (optional)
  Every push to the repo builds and tests the installer on Windows (GitHub
  Actions), and every version tag publishes it as a release. To build on your
  own PC instead, run scripts\build_exe.bat from the source code.

USE
  VodClipper has four sections in the left sidebar: Home, Review, Exports and
  Settings. The status bar at the bottom shows what's running in the background.

  Home: start a project
    Past broadcast   paste a VOD link (twitch.tv/videos/...) or Browse to a video
                     on your PC, then Find clips.
                     Clips to suggest and Clip length are Automatic by default:
                     the number scales with how long the stream is and how much
                     happened, and each clip gets its own length (15-90 s).
                     Deep scan (optional) also transcribes the whole stream to
                     find quiet gems (see below).
    Live channel     enter a channel name (or twitch.tv/name) and press Start live
                     clipping while the stream is on air.

  Review: decide what to keep
    The suggested clips are listed on the left. The middle shows the selected
    clip exactly as the TikTok will look (layout, title, word-by-word captions).
    The inspector on the right has four tabs:
      Clip       title, post caption and trim
      Captions   every caption line with its time. Click a line to jump to it,
                 type over it to fix a word (Enter to save), x to delete it,
                 or Add caption at playhead to write your own. Undo all edits
                 brings back what Whisper heard.
      Text       caption style (Pop, Highlight, Karaoke, Glow, Clean), title
                 style (white, dark or colour box, outline), fonts, size and
                 highlight colour
      Layout     cam + game, full crop or blur fit, facecam box, zoom
    Drag the title or the captions on the preview to move them; a line shows
    when they're centred, and the shaded areas are covered by TikTok's own
    buttons. Text > Reset positions puts them back.
    The hype timeline along the bottom shows the whole stream; click a
    highlighted range to jump to that clip, or (for VODs) click anywhere else to
    preview that moment and make a clip there.
      Keep and render (Enter)   renders it in the background
      Skip (X)                  sets it aside (Skipped tab)
    The header shows how many strong moments the stream has (for example
    "about 21 strong moments in this 7-hour stream"). What you keep, skip and
    post teaches VodClipper what you like for each streamer; after about eight
    decisions the header says "Tuned to your picks" (Settings > Clip ranking).
    Gems tab (after a Deep scan): quiet gems, lines that landed (the streamer
    laughed after, chat reacted, the voice changed, a punchline) even though
    chat and volume stayed calm. Deep scan can also be started from the
    header, and you can keep reviewing while it runs.
      Up / Down arrows          previous / next clip
      Space play/pause, T new title, M mute, Left/Right seek, C clip (live)
    Each clip has a number (#7) that stays with that moment, even when you
    change the sliders. Clips you kept, skipped, trimmed or retitled never drop
    out of the list. Your review is saved as you go: open the same VOD again
    (Recent on the Home screen) and everything is where you left it.

  Live mode
    Pick a mode on the Home screen:
      Hands-free         every moment is titled, checked and rendered by itself
                         with your Text and Layout style. Finished clips appear
                         in Exports while you watch. (Moments the content check
                         flags as serious are held back for you in Review.)
      Review each clip   moments wait in Review; you keep the ones you want.
    How many clips: Fewer, Normal, More or Lots. You can change it while live.
    Wait for the stream to start: start before the channel goes live and
    recording begins by itself. Keep watching: after the stream ends,
    VodClipper waits for the channel's next stream and starts again.
    Send finished clips to my TikTok drafts (hands-free, optional): each clip
    is uploaded to your TikTok inbox; you finish the post in the TikTok app.
    TikTok only allows a few pending drafts a day.
      Ctrl+Alt+C          clip the last 30 seconds from ANY window (a sound
                          confirms it). Turn it off in Settings > Live clipping.
      Clip last 30s (C)   the same, from inside VodClipper
      Stop                stop recording; you can keep reviewing afterwards
    Only the last 15 minutes of raw stream are kept on disk; moments are cut out
    as they happen, so long streams don't fill your drive. If the connection
    drops it reconnects, and it notices when the stream ends.
    Ads: Twitch stitches ads into live streams for viewers without Turbo.
    VodClipper recognises those ad breaks and leaves them out of the recording,
    so they never end up in a clip (the header shows "Ads skipped"). Sign in
    under Settings > Twitch sign-in with an account that has Turbo (or a sub to
    the channel) and Twitch sends the ad-free stream instead.

  Exports: finished clips and posting
    Every kept clip renders here, named after its title, with its post caption
    saved next to it as a .txt. Select one to watch it and post it to TikTok.
    If a clip fails to render, its row says why (for example "your drive is
    full") and what to do, with a Retry button. Retry failed retries them all.
    A retry replaces its own file instead of making a "(2)" copy, and every
    finished file is checked before it shows as done. Exports and any renders
    that hadn't finished are kept when you close VodClipper; unfinished
    renders start again the next time you open it.

CONTENT CHECK (Settings > Content check)
  Checks what each clip says, its title and its post caption for things that
  get TikTok videos removed or held back: hate slurs, self-harm and
  harassment, personal info (phone numbers, emails, street addresses),
  violence, sexual terms, drugs, swearing, gambling, and your own blocked
  words (your real name, your town...). Flags show on each clip.
  When something is flagged: just flag it, hide it in captions ("f***"),
  mute it, or bleep it. Choose which kinds get censored in the Censor column.
  It reads words only. It can't see the picture or recognise music.

  Auto titles
    VodClipper listens to each clip (Whisper) and reads how chat reacted, then
    writes a title: usually the best line said near the peak, otherwise one that
    fits the mood (funny, hype, surprise, fail, wholesome). The refresh button
    next to the title (or T) cycles through other ideas, and you can type your
    own. Each clip also gets a post caption with hashtags, which you can edit.

POSTING TO TIKTOK
  TikTok only lets registered apps post, so you set up your own (free) TikTok
  developer app once. Settings > TikTok walks you through it:
    1. Create an app at developers.tiktok.com.
    2. Add the Login Kit and Content Posting API products; turn on Direct Post.
    3. In Login Kit, pick the Desktop platform and add the redirect URI shown
       in Settings (http://127.0.0.1:*/callback/).
    4. Paste the Client key and Client secret into Settings and press
       Save and connect. Sign in to TikTok in the browser window that opens.
  Then, in Exports, select a clip, check the caption, choose who can view it,
  and press Post (or Send to drafts to finish posting in the TikTok app).
  Until TikTok reviews your developer app, direct posts can only be private
  ("Only me"); that's TikTok's rule for new apps. Drafts work either way.
  VodClipper never posts on its own; every post is a click you make.

FREEING UP SPACE
  Downloaded VODs are big (several GB per stream). Settings > Storage shows
  how much space VodClipper is using:
    Delete VODs       removes downloaded streams (and their chat/thumbnails).
                      Your rendered clips are kept. Videos of your own that you
                      opened with Browse are never deleted.
    Delete all clips  removes every rendered TikTok clip. VODs are kept.
  Both ask for confirmation first.

HOW CLIPS ARE PICKED
  - Chat: sudden surges in how many people are chatting, weighted for KEKW,
    LUL, POG, "clip it", ????? and similar. Chat reacts a few seconds late, so
    it's shifted back (Home > Past broadcast > Advanced > Chat delay).
    It counts chatters, not messages (one person spamming counts once), and
    ignores bots, !commands and giveaway entries, sub and gift-sub notices,
    copypasta, and the floods after gift bombs and raids.
  - Audio: moments much louder than the few minutes around them. Twitch's
    muted (DMCA) sections are ignored instead of counted as boring.
  - Laughter, screams and cheering, heard by a small built-in sound detector
    (Advanced > Listen for laughter, on by default; it adds about a minute per
    hour of video). Loud music counts for less.
  - Where a clip starts and ends: far enough back to show the setup, on until
    the reaction dies down, 15 to 90 seconds, and moved to the nearest pause
    between sentences once the clip has been listened to.
  - Your own keeps, skips and posts, per streamer (Settings > Clip ranking,
    where you can also make it forget).
  - With no chat log it ranks by audio only.
  - Local video? Put its chat next to it as <videoname>.chat.json, or choose
    the file under Home > Past broadcast > Advanced. TwitchDownloader chat JSON works.

CAPTIONS
  Made with Whisper. The first render with captions downloads the model
  (a few hundred MB). "Balanced" accuracy is a solid default. With an NVIDIA GPU and
  CUDA 12 + cuDNN installed, Whisper uses the GPU; otherwise the CPU.
  Rendering uses your GPU's video encoder (NVIDIA, Intel or AMD) when it can,
  and falls back to the CPU on its own.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  - Twitch downloads suddenly fail: Settings > Downloads and tools > Update downloader.
    No rebuild needed.
  - Subscriber-only VOD: sign in under Settings > Twitch sign-in. (Or, the older
    way: Settings > Downloads and tools > Use cookies from > the browser where
    you're logged in to Twitch; close that browser first.)
  - Ads still pause live recordings: that's expected without Turbo or a sub;
    the ads are left out of the clips either way.
  - The window doesn't open: VodClipper falls back to your web browser.
    Installing Microsoft's "WebView2 Runtime" brings the app window back.
  - Preview won't play: some files (MKV, HEVC) can't play in the preview.
    Finding and rendering clips still works.
  - Something else broke: send the log file at
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\VodClipper\vodclipper.log

Only clip VODs you own or have permission to repost.
